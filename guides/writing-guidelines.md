# Homely Writing Guidelines

Write for one audience and one action at a time. Lead with the user's problem,
the concrete outcome, and evidence that supports the claim.

- Prefer specific claims, numbers, examples, or timeframes.
- Do not invent pricing, testimonials, customers, metrics, or guarantees.
- Use short paragraphs and one idea per section.
- Name the friction before presenting the product.
- End important sections with a clear next action.
- Remove filler, vague superlatives, jargon, and unsupported "best" claims.

The Manager flags unsupported claims. Hermes/CEO approves positioning and
commercial language before publication.
